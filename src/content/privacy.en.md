# Privacy policy

Draft, last updated 3 October 2026. Mizan is provided by {{operator}}. Questions: {{email}}.

## In short

- You can use Mizan without an account. Your money data then stays on your device.
- If you turn on sync, your data is **end-to-end encrypted** on your device before it is uploaded. We store it but cannot read it.
- No adverts, no tracking, no cookies. Anonymous usage counts are sent only if you turn them on.

## Data on your device

Transactions, accounts, bills, budgets, goals, receipts and settings are stored in your browser or app on your device. With an app lock they are encrypted with a key that only your PIN or passkey can unlock. We have no access to them.

## If you use sync

- **Email address:** to sign you in (we send a one-time code) and to contact you about your account.
- **Encrypted data:** your records, encrypted with a key that never leaves your devices. We see only their size and when they change.
- **Devices:** a name such as "Edge on Windows" and when each device last synced, so you can sign devices out.
- **Passkeys for sign-in:** a public key; your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.
- **Reminders:** if you turn on notifications, the time of each reminder and a generic sentence such as "A bill is due tomorrow", never names or amounts.
- **Households:** who belongs to which household. What you share is encrypted with a key only members have.

## Bank connections

If you connect a bank, an authorised account information provider (GoCardless Bank Account Data) reads your balances and transactions with your consent, which lasts 90 days. Transactions pass through our server on their way to your device and are **not stored** there. We keep the bank's name, the connection status and when consent expires.

## Usage counts (optional)

If you turn on "Share anonymous usage counts", the app sends events such as "app opened" or "transaction added". We store daily totals only, with no identifier, IP address or content.

## Feedback

When you send feedback we receive your message, the app version and, if you give it, your email address.

## Legal bases (UK and EU GDPR)

- Providing sync and your account: **contract**.
- Bank connections, notifications and usage counts: your **consent**, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.
- Keeping the service secure (for example rate limits and error logs): our **legitimate interests**.

## Where data is kept and who helps us

Our servers are in London, United Kingdom. Processors: our hosting and database providers, Resend (sign-in emails), GoCardless (bank connections). They process data only on our instructions.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your sync account (Settings → Sync and devices → Delete sync account), which removes everything on our servers at once. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Error logs are kept for 30 days and contain no financial data.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Settings → Backup) and delete your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Contact {{email}}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (UK) or your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

## Children

Mizan is not intended for children under 16.

## Changes

We will show significant changes in the app before they take effect.
