# Help

How everything in Mizan works, with examples. Tap a topic to open it, or search above.

## Getting started {#start}

On the welcome screen, choose **Set up my account** to start with your own money, or **Explore with demo data** to try Mizan with made-up accounts and bills first.

1. Enter your current account's balance today and give the account a name.
2. Choose your **payday** (the day of the month you are paid).
3. Tap **Start**. A short tour shows the main screens.

> **Example:** your Monzo account has £850 today and you are paid on the 25th. Enter 850, name it "Monzo", choose 25, and Mizan works out what you can spend until the 25th.

You can change all of this later in **Settings**. To leave the demo, use **Settings → Erase all data**.

## Safe to spend {#safe}

The big number on **Home** is what you can spend until your next payday without missing a bill:

- your **everyday accounts'** balance,
- minus the **bills still due before payday**,
- minus the **savings** you set aside (**Settings → Pay cycle → Savings**).

Spread over the days left, it also shows roughly how much you can spend **per day**. Tap the number to see how it is worked out.

> **Example:** £1,200 in your current account, £400 of bills before payday and £200 put aside for savings leaves £600 safe to spend. With 10 days to payday, that is about £60 a day.

Only accounts marked **Everyday account** count. Savings, pensions and loans do not.

## Accounts {#accounts}

**Accounts** (just below Home; on a phone, the first shortcut on Home) lists every account with its value, grouped as Everyday, Savings and investments, and Debts, with your net worth at the top. Tap an account to edit it, or **Add account** to create one. Choose a type: **Current account**, **Savings**, **Credit card**, **Cash**, **Loan**, **Mortgage**, **Investments**, **Pension** or **Property**.

1. Enter a name and the **opening** balance (what it holds today).
2. Turn on **Everyday account** for accounts you spend from, so they count in "safe to spend".
3. For a credit card, add the **Credit limit**, **Interest rate (APR %)**, **Statement day**, **Payment due day** and **Minimum payment**. For a loan or mortgage, add the rate and **Monthly payment**.
4. Choose the account's **Currency** if it is not your home currency.

> **Example:** add an Amex **Credit card** with a £3,000 limit, 22.9% APR and payment due on the 15th. Mizan reminds you before the 15th and shows the card on **Net worth**.

To close an account, edit it and **Archive** it: its history stays, and you can restore it later.

Turn off **Include in net worth** for money you hold for someone else, such as a child's Junior ISA: it stays in your accounts and transfers, but is listed apart on **Net worth** and left out of its total.

## Adding a transaction {#transactions}

Tap **+** (on a computer, **Add transaction**). Choose **Expense**, **Income** or **Transfer**, then enter the amount and the **Payee**.

- Mizan suggests a **category** from your rules, your history and well-known shops. Change it if it is wrong; Mizan learns from your corrections.
- Change the date, account, **Note** or **Tags** if you need to.
- To change or delete a transaction later, tap it in **Activity**. Deleting can be undone from the message that appears.

> **Example:** a £4.50 coffee: tap **+**, type 4.50, payee "Pret". Mizan picks **Eating out**. Tap **Save expense**.

> **Example:** your salary: choose **Income**, £2,450 from "Acme Ltd", category **Salary**.

## Transfers between your accounts {#transfers}

Moving money between two of your own accounts is a **Transfer**, not spending: choose **Transfer** in the add screen, then the **From** and **To** accounts. Transfers do not count in spending, budgets or reports.

> **Example:** moving £200 to savings: **Transfer**, £200, from Current account to Savings.

> **Example:** paying off your credit card: a transfer from your current account to the card.

If the two accounts have different currencies, enter the amount that arrived as well.

**Regular transfers.** For money you move on a schedule, such as savings or a Junior ISA, go to **Bills → +** and choose **Transfer**: set the amount, how often, the next date, and the **From** and **To** accounts. With **Record it automatically on each due date** on, each transfer is added to both accounts when it falls due (turn it off if it already arrives through a bank connection or import, and tap **Mark paid** instead). If it moves money out of your everyday accounts, "safe to spend" sets it aside before payday.

> **Example:** £50 into "Junior ISA – Sara" on the 1st of every month: **Transfer**, £50, **Monthly**, next on the 1st, from Current account to Junior ISA – Sara. On the 1st, both accounts show it.

## Splitting one payment across categories {#split}

When one payment covers several things, use **Split across categories** in the add screen and divide the amount.

> **Example:** £80 at Tesco: £60 **Groceries** and £20 **Shopping** (a kettle). Each part counts in its own budget.

Split pieces show a **Split** tag in Activity. To share a cost with other people instead, see **Friends**.

## Bills and recurring payments {#bills}

Bills are payments that repeat: direct debits, standing orders and subscriptions. Go to **Bills → +** (or **Add bill**).

1. Enter the amount and a name.
2. Choose how often: **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Set **Next due** (the date of the next payment). It also sets the day it repeats on.
4. Choose the method: **Direct debit**, **Standing order** or **Card subscription**, and the account and category.
5. Optional: **Ends** for a payment that stops (such as a loan), and **Free trial ends** for a subscription on trial.

> **Example:** Council Tax: £148, **Monthly**, next due 15 October, **Direct debit**, ends next March.

> **Example:** a TV licence: £174.50, **Yearly**. A yearly bill appears again as its date gets closer.

> **Example:** a streaming service on a free trial: £10.99, **Card subscription**, **Free trial ends** on the 20th. Mizan reminds you before the trial ends so you can cancel.

**Where bills show:**

- **Bills → Upcoming**: everything due in the next 30 days, overdue bills, and what you have paid this month. **All** lists every bill with its next date; **Subscriptions** shows card subscriptions and what they cost a year.
- **Activity**: bills due in the next 7 days appear under **Coming up**, above your transactions. They join the list of transactions once paid.
- **Home** and **Calendar**: upcoming bills, and their effect on "safe to spend".

**Paying a bill:** when the payment arrives (added by hand, imported or from a bank connection), Mizan matches it to the bill automatically when it lands within 3 days of the due date. You can also tap **Mark paid**.

Mizan flags **price rises** (for example "Octopus Energy went up by £9.00") and suggests regular payments that are not bills yet: tap **Add as bill**. To stop a bill for a while, edit it and turn on **Paused**; to remove it, delete it.

## Budgets {#budgets}

Go to **Budgets → Edit** and enter a monthly limit for each category you want to keep an eye on. Leave a category empty for no budget.

- The ring shows how much of your total budget is used, and each category shows what is left.
- In **Settings → Budgets**, choose the **Budget period**: **Calendar month**, or **Payday to payday** to match your pay. Turn on **Carry over what is left** to move unspent (or overspent) money into the next period.
- You are told at 80% and 100% of a budget.

> **Example:** Groceries £300. After spending £240 you get a "nearly used" alert; at £300 the budget shows as over.

In a household, each household gets its own tab on **Budgets**: those budgets count only spending on shared accounts, and both of you can change them.

## Categories, rules and payee names {#categories}

**Settings → Categories**: add your own categories, choose a **Colour**, change their order, or archive one (its transactions, bills and rules **Move to** another category).

**Settings → Rules and payee names**:

- A **rule** sets the category automatically: "payee **contains** Tesco → Groceries". Tap **Apply to past** to fix older transactions too.
- **Rename a payee** to tidy up bank descriptions: "AMZN MKTP UK*2X4" → "Amazon".

> **Example:** add the rule "contains Uber → Transport". Every Uber trip, typed or imported, lands in Transport.

## Goals {#goals}

**Goals → New goal**: a name, a **Target** and, if you like, a date to reach it **By**. Use **Add money** when you put money aside, and **Take out** if you use some.

> **Example:** "Summer holiday", target £1,500 by June. Mizan shows how much to put aside each month to get there on time, and celebrates when you reach it.

## Importing a bank statement {#import}

Download a CSV file from your bank's website, then go to **Import → Choose a CSV file**.

1. Check the **Columns** (date, description, amount) and the date **Format**. Mizan recognises most UK and French banks.
2. **Amounts**: **One column** (money out is negative) or **Separate money in and out**. If money out shows as positive, tick **Flip signs**.
3. Check the **Preview**: rows already in Mizan are marked **Already imported** and skipped, and bill payments are matched to their bills.
4. Tap **Import**. Changed your mind? **Recent imports → Undo import**.

> **Tip:** set up rules first, so imported transactions land in the right categories.

## Bank connections {#banks}

With sync turned on, **Settings → Bank connections → Connect a bank** brings your transactions in automatically.

1. Choose your **Country** and bank.
2. Confirm on your bank's own website or app, then come back to Mizan.
3. Choose which Mizan account each bank account goes into, or **Don't import**.
4. New transactions arrive by themselves; tap **Get new transactions** to check now.

Banks ask you to renew the connection every 90 days: Mizan reminds you, and you tap **Reconnect**. **Match the bank** sets the account's balance to the bank's. **Disconnect** stops it at any time.

## Receipts and documents {#receipts}

Open a transaction and tap **Add photo or PDF** to keep a receipt with it. **Read receipt** fills in the amount and date from a photo, on your device: the picture is never sent anywhere.

Add a **Return by** or **Warranty until** date and Mizan reminds you before the return window closes or the warranty ends.

> **Example:** new headphones for £149: attach the receipt and set **Return by** in 30 days and **Warranty until** in two years.

## Search and editing many at once {#search}

**Activity → Advanced search** finds transactions by **Text** (payee, note or tag), dates (**From**, **To**), amount (**At least**, **At most**), category, account and **Money in** or **Money out**. **Save this search** keeps it for later.

Tap **Select**, choose transactions, then **Edit selected** to **Move to category**, **Add tag**, **Remove tag** or **Delete selected**.

> **Example:** tag a whole trip: search "Lisbon" from 1 to 8 May, **Select all shown**, **Add tag** "Holiday 2027".

## Reports {#reports}

**Reports** shows **Spending by category**, **Income and spending by month**, **Top payees**, your **Savings rate** and a **Balance forecast** that warns if you may run low. Choose the period at the top, including **Custom** dates and a comparison **Compared with before**. **Category trends** show the last 12 months and your monthly average.

- **Year in review**: your year at a glance; **Print or save as PDF**.
- **Tax helper**: when adding income or an expense, choose a heading under **Tax** (for example **Self-employment income**). The helper totals them per tax year and **Export CSV** gives your accountant a list.

## Calendar {#calendar}

**Calendar** shows bills, paydays and your expected balance day by day. Tap a day to see what was paid, what is due, and the transactions on it.

## Net worth and debts {#networth}

**Net worth** is everything you own (**Assets**) minus everything you owe (**Debts**), with its history over 12 months. For a house or investments, tap **Update value** from time to time.

The **Debt payoff planner** shows when each loan and card is paid off. Enter what you can pay **Each month** and choose a **Strategy**: **Avalanche** (highest interest first, cheapest overall) or **Snowball** (smallest balance first, quick wins). **What if I paid extra** shows the time and interest you save.

> **Example:** a £2,000 card at 22.9% and a £6,000 car loan at 7%: with £400 a month, Avalanche clears the card first and saves the most interest.

Accounts with **Include in net worth** turned off appear under **Not counted in net worth**, where you can still update their value.

## Friends: splitting costs {#friends}

**Friends → Add a friend**, with an optional **Payment link** (such as a Monzo.me or PayPal.me link).

1. When adding an expense, tap your friends' names under **Split with friends**. The cost is shared equally.
2. **Friends** then shows who owes whom.
3. **Request** opens their payment link with the amount, or **Copy a reminder** to send it yourself. When they pay, tap **Settle up**.

> **Example:** pizza night for £30 with Jamie: add the expense, tap Jamie, and Mizan notes "1 friend owes you £15.00".

## Household: sharing with a partner {#household}

With sync on, **Settings → Household → Create a household**, then **Invite someone**: send the link (it works once, for a week). Choose the **Shared accounts**; everything else stays private.

Shared accounts, their transactions and their bills appear for both of you, end-to-end encrypted. Each household also gets a **Budgets** tab. **Leave household** at any time: your accounts become private again.

> **Example:** you share the joint current account and its bills with your partner, but your own savings stay yours alone.

## Notifications {#notifications}

**Settings → Notifications**: turn on reminders for this device and choose which ones: bills due tomorrow, payday, budgets at 80% and 100%, a balance that may run low, unusual payments, free trials ending, yearly renewals, credit card payments, return windows, warranties and bank connections expiring.

Set **Quiet from** and **Quiet until** so nothing arrives at night, and **Send a test notification** to check. All alerts also appear in the bell on **Home**.

## News by email {#news}

Want to hear about new features? Go to **Settings → News by email**. You can also tick the box when you turn on sync, or subscribe on our website. The box is never ticked for you.

- **Signed in to sync:** switch news on or off; your address is already confirmed.
- **Not signed in:** enter your email and tap **Subscribe**, then click the link we email you to confirm it.

Every email has an unsubscribe link: one click and you are off the list. You can also switch news off in Settings at any time.

> **Example:** you tick "Email me news about Mizan" when you turn on sync. A few times a year you get an email about what is new; one day you tap **Unsubscribe** at the bottom, confirm, and that is the last one.

## Sync and devices {#sync}

Mizan works without an account: your data stays on your device. To use it on your phone and your computer, turn on **Settings → Sync and devices**.

1. Enter your email and the 6-digit code you receive.
2. **Save your recovery key** in a password manager or on paper. It unlocks your data on another device; nobody can reset it for you.
3. On your other device, open Mizan, choose **Already use Mizan? Sign in to sync**, and enter the same email, the code and the recovery key.

Everything is **end-to-end encrypted**: the server stores your data but cannot read it. **Signed-in devices** lists your devices; **Sign out** removes one, for example a lost phone.

## Security and privacy {#security}

**Settings → Security**:

- **App lock**: a PIN of 4 to 8 digits keeps others out and encrypts your data on this device. Choose when it locks (**Lock after**) and use **Lock now** to lock straight away.
- **Passkey**: unlock with your fingerprint, face or security key.
- **Hide amounts**: shows £••• instead of amounts, for when others can see your screen.

There are no adverts and no tracking. Anonymous usage counts are only sent if you turn them on in **Settings → About**.

## Backup and export {#backup}

**Settings → Backup → Download backup** saves all your data in one file. Add a password to encrypt it. **Restore from backup** brings it back on any device. **Export transactions (CSV)** opens your transactions in a spreadsheet.

Without sync, your data lives only on this device: download a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe.

## Language and currencies {#language}

**Settings → Language and currency**: choose English, French or Arabic, your currency, the **Digits** (Latin or Arabic-Indic) and which day the **Week starts on**.

Each account can have its own **Currency**. Totals, budgets and "safe to spend" are shown in your home currency, using daily exchange rates you can check and adjust.

> **Example:** a euro account with €500 shows in its own currency, and counts as about £430 in your totals.

## Lost a device or forgot your PIN? {#lost}

**Lost device:** from another signed-in device, **Settings → Sync and devices → Signed-in devices → Sign out** the lost one. On a new device, sign in to sync with your email and recovery key: everything comes back. Without sync, restore your latest backup file.

**Forgot your PIN:** the PIN encrypts your data, so it cannot be reset. Unlock with a passkey if you added one. Otherwise clear this site's data in your browser (or reinstall the app), then sign in to sync again with your recovery key, or restore a backup.
