# Help

## What does "safe to spend" mean?

Your everyday accounts' balance, minus bills still due before your next payday, minus the savings you set aside. Spread over the days to payday, it shows roughly what you can spend each day.

## How do I add a transaction?

Tap **+** (or **Add transaction** on a computer). Ledger suggests a category from your rules, your history and well-known shops. Attach a receipt photo and Ledger can read the amount and date from it.

## How do bills work?

Add direct debits, standing orders and subscriptions under **Bills**. Payments are matched to bills automatically, price rises are flagged, and you are reminded the day before a bill is due. Regular payments that are not bills yet are suggested.

## Can I import my bank statement?

Yes: **Import** reads CSV files from most UK and French banks. Duplicates are skipped and payments are matched to bills. With sync on you can also connect a bank under **Settings → Bank connections**.

## How do I use Ledger on several devices?

Turn on **Settings → Sync and devices**. Sign in with an emailed code, then save your **recovery key**: you need it to add another device. Your data is encrypted before it leaves the device.

## I forgot my PIN

The PIN encrypts your data on this device, so it cannot be reset. Unlock with a passkey if you added one. Otherwise clear this site's data in your browser and restore a backup, or sign in to sync again with your recovery key.

## How do I keep my data safe?

Download a backup regularly (**Settings → Backup**), ideally with a password. Use a PIN or a passkey, and **Hide amounts** when others can see your screen.

## Can I share with my partner?

Yes: **Settings → Household**. Invite them with a link and choose which accounts to share. Everything else stays private. To split costs with friends, use **Friends**.

## Languages and currencies

Choose English, French or Arabic and your currency in **Settings → Language and currency**. Each account can hold its own currency; totals are converted to your home currency.
